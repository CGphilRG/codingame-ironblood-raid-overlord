The Goal

Solo Optimization puzzle. Command the dark Overlord and your tactical Runic River across a fortified village of interconnected Zones: repel the raiding Hero AI squad, guard your Sanctum Boss and secret Captive until maxRounds rounds expire (or eliminate every Hero), and maximize damage, Hero eliminations, and gem efficiency!

 Rules

The siege plays over 8 or 9 rounds. Each round, the Hero AI squad acts first, then your Overlord Turn executes.

Zones, Line of Sight & Hindrance

  • The board consists of zoneCount planar Zones linked by orthogonal passages (nNorth, nEast, nSouth, nWest; -1 means a blocked wall).
  • You know the exact hidden Captive location (captiveZone) from round 1, whereas the Hero AI must discover it by searching Huts.
  • Line of Sight (LoS): Valid for range-2 attacks (ARCHER, BOSS) if same zone, adjacent neighbor, or via one shared non-Hut zone mid when target is not a Hut (zoneType = 1).
  • Hindrance: Leaving a zone with H alive Heroes costs an enemy 1 + H movement points. Conversely, every awake enemy in a Hero's zone adds +1 movement cost when that Hero tries to leave!

The Runic River Conveyor & Overlord Economy

  • At the start of your Overlord turn, recover up to recoverRate gems from overlordFatigue into overlordReserve.
  • Activate (0..2 tiles per turn): Use ACTIVATE tileId to pay the tile's current 1-based slotCost (1..riverLength) from overlordReserve. The activated tile shifts to the rightmost (costliest) slot, sliding all tiles behind it 1 slot left (cheaper)!
  • Slumbering tiles (tileState = 1) wake immediately upon activation. Defeated tiles (tileState = 2) cannot be activated, but clog the River until removed or reinforced.
  • Dredging (2 gems): Use DREDGE tileId on any defeated tile (tileState = 2) to permanently remove it from the River for 2 gems—sliding remaining tiles left, at the cost of never reinforcing that tile again.
  • EVENT & Reinforcements: Activating the EVENT tile grants reinforcePower reinforcement points for that turn. Spend them with REINFORCE tileId spawnZoneId to return slain minions (reinforceCost each) onto any Reinforcement zone (zoneType = 4).

Unexecutable Actions & Viewer

  • Valid-syntax commands whose target or budget fails (activating a 3rd tile, dredging an alive tile, attacking out of LoS) are safely ignored.
  • Use the viewer's RENDER MODE toggle to switch between RPG SPRITES and GEOMETRIC DEBUG.
Victory Conditions
  • Eliminate all Heroes or prevent them from escaping with both the Captive and the Boss slain by the end of round maxRounds.
  • Score Criterion: 1000 + 35×Hero wounds + 350×Heroes slain + 250 (Boss alive) + Captive security bonus (200 hidden / 100 dropped) + 15×remaining Overlord reserve.
Defeat Conditions
  • The Hero AI kills your Sanctum Boss and escapes through the Exit carrying the Captive.
  • Your program times out or outputs an invalid / malformed command.

 Game Input

Initialization Input

Line 1: 9 space-separated integers role, maxRounds, cols, rows, zoneCount, heroCount, tileCount, recoverRate, reinforcePower.

Next zoneCount lines: 8 space-separated integers zoneId, gx, gy, zoneType, nNorth, nEast, nSouth, nWest.

Next heroCount lines: 9 space-separated values initHeroId, archetype, totalGems, freeMove, meleeTier, rangedTier, heroArmor, exertMelee, exertRanged.

Next tileCount lines: 9 space-separated values initTileId, unitType, maxModels, maxHp, tileArmor, atkPower, atkRange, movePts, reinforceCost.

Input for One Game Turn

Line 1: 5 space-separated integers round, captiveState, captiveZone (exact hut ID when hidden), overlordReserve (before start-of-turn recovery), overlordFatigue.

Next heroCount lines: 8 space-separated integers heroId, alive, heroZone, reserve, fatigue, wounds, stance, carrying.

Next tileCount lines: 5 space-separated integers tileId, slotCost, tileState, dredged, aliveModels.

Next line: activeEnemyCount, followed by activeEnemyCount lines of 5 integers: enemyId, enemyTileId, enemyZone, enemyHp, slumbering.

Next line: poiCount, followed by poiCount lines of 3 integers: poiZone, poiKind, poiState.

Output for One Game Turn

A single line containing semicolon-separated commands executed in order:

  • DREDGE tileId (2 gems) to permanently remove a dead tile from the River.
  • ACTIVATE tileId (slotCost gems, max 2/turn) to ready all models of a tile or trigger EVENT.
  • MOVE enemyId zoneId to move an activated enemy one connected zone.
  • ATTACK enemyId heroId to strike a Hero in range/LoS.
  • REINFORCE tileId spawnZoneId to respawn a slain minion onto a zoneType=4 zone.
  • PASS to hold reserve gems without acting.

Example: DREDGE 0; ACTIVATE 2; MOVE 4 5; ATTACK 4 0; MSG Ambush!

Constraints
12 ≤ zoneCount ≤ 16, 2 ≤ heroCount ≤ 3, 6 ≤ tileCount ≤ 8
8 ≤ maxRounds ≤ 9
Response time first turn ≤ 1000 ms, per turn ≤ 50 ms
